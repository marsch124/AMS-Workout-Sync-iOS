AMS Triathlon Training 2.17 (93) - what to test

This version has two new things: a strength session now also ticks itself from your Garmin watch, and Progress counts your strength sessions for the month. Say what you saw if anything looks wrong.

1. On the Garmin watch, record a Strength activity of 10 minutes or more on a day that has a planned strength session in Triathlon (do not use Workout Companion for it). Let it sync to Garmin Connect. Then open Triathlon on a device where your Garmin key is saved (iPhone, iPad or Mac).

2. The strength session of that day should now be done by itself, with the activity's minutes as its time. Nothing to tap. A grey line should say Ticked from Garmin, on Today and on the session's page. No effort is filled in; How hard was it? can still be asked. It can take up to 10 minutes before the app asks Garmin again.

3. Open Progress and look at the Load card. Under the Mobility line there should be a grey line like Strength: 3 of 4 this month. It counts this month's planned strength sessions up to today: how many are done, out of how many were planned.

4. Open the Garmin-ticked session and clear its log. Close the app, open it again, and look on another device too. The session should stay cleared and not be ticked again.

5. Workout Companion still comes first: on the iPhone, finish a workout in Workout Companion on a day with a planned strength session. The grey line should say Ticked from Workout Companion, not Ticked from Garmin.

6. Things that should stay as they are: a session you marked missed is left alone, a day ahead is never ticked, only the first strength session still to do on a day is ticked, and Mobility is still ticked from Magic Morning only. Without a Garmin key nothing changes.

7. Undo, only if you want it: open the session and change or clear the log.

Only your own devices can prove these: the real watch activity in Garmin Connect, and the iCloud arrival on the iPad and the Mac.
