AMS Triathlon Training 2.15 (91) - what to test

This version has one new thing: a mobility session ticks itself when you stretched in Magic Morning. Say what you saw if anything looks wrong.

1. Do a Magic Morning routine on a Monday, Wednesday or Friday, with a stretching step that takes a minute or more.

2. Open Triathlon on the iPhone. The planned mobility session of that day should now be ticked by itself, with the stretching minutes as its time. Nothing to tap.

3. Look at the session on Today and on its page. A grey line should say Ticked from Magic Morning.

4. A strength session should be as before: Done in Workouts with the button Log it, and Logged from Workouts after your tap. It is never ticked by itself.

5. A morning without stretching should tick nothing. A mobility session you already logged or marked missed should be left as it is. A day ahead should never be ticked.

6. After a little while the same tick should show on your iPad and your Mac.

7. Undo, only if you want it: open the session and change or clear the log. It should not tick again.

8. Progress should still say Stretching: N of the last 7 mornings, as before.

Only your own devices can prove these: the real Magic Morning note on your iPhone, and the iCloud arrival on the iPad and the Mac.
