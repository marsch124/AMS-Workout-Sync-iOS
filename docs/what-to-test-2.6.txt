New: a Body page for the numbers from your Garmin watch. On an iPhone it is the middle tab, between Sessions and Progress. On an iPad and a Mac it is a row in the side list.

Please look at:
- The raspberry card: Body Battery now, the highest today, and the curve of the day with your workouts on it.
- The four tiles: Sleep, HRV, Resting pulse and Stress, each with seven slim bars. Tap a tile to see its 7 days in numbers.
- Pull down to read Garmin again.
- With no Garmin key saved, the page should offer Open Garmin settings.
- Day and night, iPhone and Mac.

It is shown only: it changes no plan and makes no suggestion. It uses the same Garmin key as the morning numbers on Today (Settings, Garmin).
