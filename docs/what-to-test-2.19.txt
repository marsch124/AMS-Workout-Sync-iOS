AMS Triathlon Training 2.19 (95) - what to test

Six small changes. Nothing in your plan changes by itself. Say what you saw if anything looks wrong.

1. Routes (iPhone, with your Garmin key): Progress > the ROUTES card. Look at the line "12 routes · 5 runs..." (your own numbers). Tap it: move and zoom the map, go back a month with the arrow, tap the chips Run / Bike / Swim / Hike (filled = shown, outlined = hidden), tap a route for its small plate. "Done" closes it.
2. Routes without a key (iPad or Mac with no key): the card says "Add your Garmin key in Settings > Garmin."
3. Sleep need: Settings > Garmin > "Sleep need" should say "6h a night - your setting". Open it, pick 7h, "Save". Body > Sleep debt should now say "Need 7h a night (your setting)". Try "Garmin's estimate" to get the old way back. Check the iPad shows the same.
4. Gym sessions: Body > Gym sessions. The big figure is now the time (like 1h 05m), not kilograms. Check the list rows and "vs the one before". Best sets still say kg.
5. Workout Companion on a free day: finish a gym session on a day with no strength planned. Open Triathlon: an Extra workout "Strength" with the note "From Workout Companion" appears on Today and under Sessions > Done. Delete it: it must not come back.
6. Extra workout button: Today, the small "+ Extra workout" is now on its own line above "Week so far", at the right.
7. Settings > Your plan: no "Thursday rides" row any more. Your Thursday rides are still in the plan; Settings > Changes to the plan still has their Undo.

Only your own devices can prove: real Garmin routes, Workout Companion on the iPhone, iCloud on the iPad and the Mac.
