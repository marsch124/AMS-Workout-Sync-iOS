One change to look at: Settings > How this works.
1. It now reads as 31 parts (before: 38 parts of long paragraphs). Each has a title and a few short lines; 25 of them also show one picture of the real screen.
2. Look at it by day and by night. The pictures follow your phone's look. They show an invented plan, never yours.
3. Busy pictures have numbered circles. Each number matches the line that starts with the same number.
4. Tap a picture, or the small "Tap to see it bigger" under it. On iPhone it opens in a sheet; on iPad and Mac it fills the screen, with the lines beside a phone picture. Pinch or double-tap to go closer. Done closes it (Escape on a Mac).
5. Signs in this app is shorter, sorted by part of the app, and has the numbered circle too.
Try it on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
Nothing else in the app changed.
