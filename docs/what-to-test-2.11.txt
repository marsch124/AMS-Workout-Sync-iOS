AMS Triathlon Training 2.11 (87) - what to test

This version has one new thing: a link to every page. Nothing on the screens changed. Say what you saw if anything looks wrong.

1. The links in Safari. Type amsworkoutsync://plan in Safari's address bar. Triathlon should open on Plan. Then try amsworkoutsync://body, amsworkoutsync://progress, amsworkoutsync://settings and amsworkoutsync://today. Each should open its own page.

2. Sessions on an iPhone. amsworkoutsync://sessions should open the Plan tab (the iPhone has no Sessions tab). On an iPad or a Mac it should open Sessions in the side list.

3. iPad and Mac. Each link should choose the matching row in the side list, even when Triathlon was already open on another page.

4. A wrong name. Try amsworkoutsync://nothing. Triathlon may come to the front but should stay as it was, with nothing changed.

5. Deck Mate (Mac 0.24). With Triathlon in front, the page group Triathlon should show. Press Plan (blue), Body (pink), Progress (purple), Settings (slate) and Today (green): each should open its own page, not only bring Triathlon to the front. Check the other keys: Training today, Body Battery, Sleep, Resting pulse, Stress, Steps, Ironman plan, Workouts, Workouts 7 days, and the key back to AMS Deck.

6. Other apps. Make a Shortcut with Open URL amsworkoutsync://progress, or type it in Raycast. It should open Progress.

7. Nothing else moved. Spotlight results still open their session, Siri's phrases still work, and Today, Body, Progress and Settings look as before.

Only your own devices can prove these: the links from Safari, Shortcuts and Raycast, the side list choice on an iPad and a Mac, and Deck Mate's Triathlon keys.
